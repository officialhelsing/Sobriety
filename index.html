Sex Differences in Daily Pharmaceutical Occupancy among U.S. Adults

Independent scholar

September 2026

Abstract

This paper operationalizes population sobriety as the preservation of an unmediated neurochemical and metabolic baseline rather than as the absence of episodic recreational intoxication. Under that definition the relevant quantity is Composite Exogenous Load: the expected number of days per year on which an adult’s monoaminergic, hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal, thyroid, incretin, or sleep circuitry is under standing pharmaceutical command. Recreational ethanol and cannabis are retained as a side ledger of event-days. They are not permitted to veto a scheduled ligand that occupies receptors on the remaining days of the year.

National interview, claims, and dispensing series from 2021 through 2025 are assembled into a parts list of qualifying agents and into a first occupancy-day envelope by sex. Qualifying agents are partitioned into three tiers. Tier 1 contains scheduled endocrine agents with no male analogue at population scale: combined and progestin-only oral contraceptives, hormonal intrauterine systems, etonogestrel implants, depot medroxyprogesterone, patches and rings, menopausal hormone therapy, endometriosis GnRH agents, and anti-estrogenic breast-cancer regimens, together with acne-dose spironolactone. Tier 2 contains shared classes with a stable female excess: mental health medication, benzodiazepines, hypnotics, levothyroxine, and weight-indication GLP-1 agonists. Adult stimulant fills, once a candidate male class, are now female-led and sit here as well. Tier 3 contains female-concentrated treatments whose denominators follow female-concentrated disease, principally CGRP migraine antagonists and antiresorptive osteoporosis drugs. Cosmetics, fragrance musks, and event-only obstetrical agents are refused.

On twelve-month mental health medication the 2024 National Health Interview Survey records 24.9 percent of women and 13.4 percent of men. On depression-specific medication the 2023 brief records 15.3 percent and 7.4 percent. Among females aged 15–49, current oral contraception stands at 11.4 percent and long-acting reversible contraception at 10.5 percent, with no male pharmaceutical counterpart. Daily or near-daily sleep-aid use in 2024 stands at 14.8 percent of women and 10.8 percent of men. Weight-indication GLP-1 use in 2025 survey series is higher among women than among men. An independence envelope built from these margins places the probability of at least one standing qualifying agent on a random day near one half for adult women and near one quarter for adult men. That envelope is not a joint distribution. It is a bound that claims files must replace. Sensitivity rows that halve twelve-month mental health medication, drop GLP-1, drop levothyroxine, restrict hormones to the pill, and add male testosterone, finasteride, and daily cannabis do not reverse the sex order under matched estimands. The one arithmetic that favors men pairs a female lower bound with a male independence envelope. That is not a matched comparison. Duty-cycle-correcting the psychotropic floor before that mixed comparison produces an all-adult male lead and an 18–44 tie. Matched envelopes in ages 18–44 remain 0.46 against 0.18.

If sobriety is unmediated baseline, adult women carry the higher per capita Composite Exogenous Load. The surplus is pharmaceutical. It is concentrated in ages 18–44, where Tier 1 steroids land on the same heads as Tier 2 psychotropics. Adult male excesses in binge drinking and cannabis use remain real after age 25 and convert to a small number of event-days per capita per year. They do not restore a native cycle, a native serotonergic set point, or an unmedicated night.

Keywords. composite exogenous load; occupancy-days; hormonal contraception; antidepressants; hypnotics; GLP-1 agonists; levothyroxine; CGRP antagonists; female-exclusive pharmacopeia; unmediated baseline

1. Introduction

A public quarrel about which sex is more sober has been conducted, until now, in the wrong units. The folk unit is the weekend. A man who drinks heavily twice a month is entered as unsober. A woman who wakes and sleeps for a year under a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor, a levonorgestrel intrauterine system, and a zolpidem prescription is entered as sober provided she is not drunk at the time of the argument. That bookkeeping treats an event as a regime and a regime as an irrelevance. It cannot decide the question it pretends to decide.

The present paper changes the unit. Sobriety is defined as the preservation of an unmediated neurochemical and metabolic baseline: the state in which hypothalamic-pituitary gonadal pulse generation, monoaminergic tone, thyroid set point, incretin signaling, and sleep architecture are not under standing exogenous command. Composite Exogenous Load is the expected count of such commands across a year, expressed first as the probability that a random adult of given sex is mediated on a random day, and second as the expected number of concurrent qualifying classes. Recreational intoxicants remain visible. They are scored as event-days. A binge is a day. A daily tablet is three hundred and sixty-five days.

The originating observation that forced the change of unit was not subtle. A large and stable female excess in antidepressant and other mental health medication has been recorded across interview and dispensing systems for years, with White adults at the top of the race distribution and with a post-2020 acceleration that is a female adolescent and young-adult phenomenon. Hormonal contraception is, at population scale, a female institution. Sleep medication used most days is more female. Levothyroxine exposure is more female. The weight-indication GLP-1 wave is more female. Spironolactone as acne therapy is prescribed to women and withheld from men because it is anti-androgenic at those doses. None of these facts is obscure. What has been obscure is the refusal to add them in a unit that respects duration.

Two errors have kept the addition from being made. The first error is clinical courtesy. A prescription is treated as the cancellation of a need rather than as the installation of a ligand. Need may be real. Ligand installation is still ligand installation. The second error is recreational capture. Because adult men still binge more and still use more cannabis after age 25, the pharmaceutical ledger is treated as secondary. Sixteen episodic ethanol days do not offset a year of serotonergic occupancy. They do not restart an ovary that has been placed under progestin.

The design that follows is deliberately coarse. No new survey is fielded. The paper takes published national prevalences, assigns each qualifying agent a duty cycle, writes the parts list by sex, and constructs an independence envelope for the union probability while stating, at every point, that the envelope is not the joint. The missing object is the concurrent class count in a thirty-day window from MEPS or a commercial claims file. Until that file is in hand the cocktail remains a stack of large female margins. The stack is already enough to reject the claim that women, on the unmediated-baseline definition, are the more sober sex.

2. Sources, inclusion rules, and the occupancy-day algebra

The paper is an accounting exercise performed on published national estimates. It assigns published prevalences to a common unit and states where the assignment is exact, where it is a duration approximation, and where it is only a bound. Readers who want a joint distribution must wait for a claims extract. Readers who want a verdict from the parts list already have enough structure to see the direction of the sex difference.

2.1 Source hierarchy

Three federal interview systems supply the core stocks. The National Health Interview Survey supplies twelve-month mental health medication, twelve-month depression medication, counseling, and the 2024 sleep-aid items that distinguish prescription hypnotics, over-the-counter sleep aids, and cannabis or cannabidiol used as a sleep aid. The National Survey of Family Growth supplies current contraceptive status among females aged 15–49, with method-specific shares for the pill, intrauterine devices, implants, injectables, and sterilization. The National Survey on Drug Use and Health supplies past-month and past-year alcohol and cannabis, binge and heavy use, and prescription tranquilizer and sedative use and misuse.

Claims and dispensing series supply slope and class detail that household interviews cannot. IQVIA longitudinal fills document the post-March 2020 steepening of adolescent antidepressant dispensing and its concentration among females. RAND American Life Panel and Gallup supply GLP-1 figures by sex and age for the weight indication. Optum and NHANES-derived hypothyroidism series supply the levothyroxine denominator. JAMA Health Forum NHANES work supplies menopausal hormone therapy prevalence. Guttmacher method tables convert NSFG percentages into user counts. Ideology and media diet are excluded from Composite Exogenous Load. They may describe a climate. They do not occupy a receptor.

2.2 What counts as standing occupancy

An agent enters the index if two conditions hold. The labeled or customary course is measured in weeks or longer. The mechanism of action alters monoaminergic tone, GABAergic tone, hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal signaling, thyroid hormone availability, incretin and satiety signaling, or sleep initiation and maintenance. A weekend of ethanol fails the first condition. A statin fails the second. Cosmetic botulinum toxin and hyaluronic fillers fail the second as indexed here, even though their procedure ratios run about 9:1 and 10:1 female. A combined oral contraceptive satisfies both. An SSRI satisfies both. Zolpidem taken most nights satisfies both. Semaglutide taken weekly for weight satisfies both. Levothyroxine satisfies both. Acne-dose spironolactone satisfies both. Tamoxifen satisfies both. Dienogest satisfies both. A CGRP monoclonal antibody satisfies both.

Duty cycle is assigned by class. Daily oral tablets and intrauterine and implant systems already in situ are scored as 365 days among current users. Weekly GLP-1 injections are scored as 365 days among current users because receptor engagement is maintained across the week. Twelve-month interview items overstate the daily stock if some users completed a short course. Sleep-aid items that specify “most days or every day in the past thirty days” are the cleanest duty-cycle measures in the interview set. Episodic female-only agents such as emergency contraception and mifepristone are occupancy events. They are not standing load and stay out of the core index.

2.3 The three estimands

Coverage is the probability that a random adult of given sex is mediated on a random day. Intensity is the expected number of concurrent qualifying classes. Occupancy-days per capita per year are coverage times 365. A woman on a pill, an SSRI, and a hypnotic contributes three to intensity and 1,095 class-days. A man who binges, using 26 percent past-month male binge prevalence and a generous five binge days in that month, contributes about sixteen ethanol event-days per adult man per year. Daily male cannabis, where it exists, does enter male coverage. It will not be allowed to stand in for a missing male contraceptive steroid.

The published sources give margins, not a joint. The lower bound on coverage is the largest single daily class prevalence. The independence upper envelope is one minus the product of the complements. Positive dependence among classes, the clinically ordinary case, lowers coverage relative to independence. Population intensity does not move. Linearity of expectation makes intensity the sum of the class prevalences whether or not the classes nest. What dependence does is concentrate that sum on fewer heads: fewer people covered, more classes each. Age restriction is not optional. The theoretically sharp contrast is ages 18–44, where Tier 1 contraception, post-2020 antidepressant slope, acne-dose spironolactone, and rising weight-indication GLP-1 inhabit the same band.

The algebra refuses to add raw percentages across classes and call the sum a prevalence. It refuses to convert occupancy into a drunkenness score with invented weights. It refuses to treat help-seeking as a deletion operator on a published fill rate. It refuses to treat a male event-day as the moral equivalent of a female standing day. Those refusals are the method.

Methods box

Coverage is 1 minus the product of the complements of each class prevalence. Occupancy-days are coverage times 365. Intensity is the sum of the class prevalences. The base envelope uses five all-adult working shares. Women: mental health medication 0.249, hormonal methods 0.09, prescription hypnotic most days 0.064, levothyroxine 0.075, weight-indication GLP-1 0.152. Men: 0.134, 0, 0.039, 0.025, 0.097. The hormone term is 0.18 of women 15–49 times about one half, the share of adult women in that age band. That 0.18 is conservative: the pill at 0.114 plus LARC at 0.105 minus a copper residual of about two points, plus smaller depot, patch, and ring stocks, rounded down. Current intrauterine systems, implants, daily tablets, and weekly GLP-1 injections are scored at duty cycle 1 among current users. Twelve-month mental health items overstate daily stock. That overstatement is a sensitivity row, not a hidden assumption. Ethanol event-days are past-month binge prevalence times five binge days in that month times twelve: about sixteen per adult man and about ten per adult woman. The five is a generous within-month frequency. Daily or almost daily alcohol (3.9 percent of persons 12 or older in 2024) is standing ethanol. It is not added on top of binge event-days. It enters Table 4 as a substitution row. Positive dependence lowers coverage relative to independence. Population intensity, the sum of class prevalences, does not move. Never compare a lower bound on one sex to an independence envelope on the other. Classes omitted from the base envelope, including daily cannabis, testosterone, finasteride, stimulants, benzodiazepines, menopausal hormone therapy, spironolactone, and nicotine, are entered only in Table 4 or named as omissions. Cosmetics, fragrance musks, and obstetric events stay out of every row.

3. Tier 1. Female-exclusive scheduled endocrine occupancy

Tier 1 is the column that ends the argument about symmetry. These agents are prescribed to women for indications that do not exist in men, or they are withheld from men because the same mechanism feminizes. Their contribution to male Composite Exogenous Load is zero at population scale. Any accounting that omits them and then declares the sexes tied on “medication” has already chosen its conclusion.

3.1 Combined and progestin-only oral contraceptives

The National Survey of Family Growth for 2022–2023 records that 54.3 percent of females aged 15–49 were current contraceptive users and that oral pills were used by 11.4 percent of all women in that age range. White non-Hispanic females show the highest current-use and pill-use rates in the NSFG brief (58.7 percent any method, 14.1 percent pill), above Black non-Hispanic and Hispanic females. The pill is therefore thicker in the same demographic cell that already leads antidepressant tables.

Combined oral contraceptives suppress pituitary gonadotropins, abolish the pre-ovulatory estradiol surge and the luteal progesterone rise, and substitute a synthetic pair whose androgenicity and sex-hormone-binding-globulin effects vary by progestin. That substitution is the occupancy. Mood heterogeneity across formulations is real and is not a male analogue. A man has no analogue. Progestin-only pills are a smaller slice of the same axis.

3.2 Hormonal intrauterine systems and the implant

Long-acting reversible contraception in NSFG 2022–2023 stands at 10.5 percent of women 15–49. Guttmacher counts about 6.8 million IUD users and about 1.9 million implant users in the same age range. Most current United States intrauterine devices in use are levonorgestrel systems rather than copper. The implant is etonogestrel. Both are multi-year progestin delivery. Duty cycle among current users is one. Removal, not adherence, is the off-switch. The implant is concentrated among younger users: about 11 percent of adolescent contraceptive users against about 1 percent of users aged 35–49.

Copper devices, male condoms attributed to female respondents, withdrawal, fertility awareness, and sterilization do not enter Composite Exogenous Load. They prevent pregnancy. They do not occupy the receptors this paper is built to count. Collapsing “contraception” into “hormones” is the error that lets a critic delete the endocrine clause. The clause is hormones.

3.3 Depot medroxyprogesterone, the patch, the ring, and later steroids

Depot medroxyprogesterone acetate is a high-occupancy progestin given every thirteen weeks. It suppresses the cycle and lowers endogenous estradiol. Current-use prevalence is smaller than the pill or the intrauterine system. Ever-use is not small. The combined patch and the vaginal ring are daily-equivalent estrogen-progestin delivery by another route. They are Tier 1 by mechanism, not by headline share.

Menopausal hormone therapy is the second female-only sex-steroid channel. Yang and Toriola, using NHANES through March 2020, place current use among postmenopausal women at 4.7 percent, down from 26.9 percent in 1999. Non-Hispanic White women remain the highest group at 5.8 percent. Spironolactone at acne doses is an anti-androgen prescribed to women and withheld from men because gynecomastia follows the mechanism. Prescribing for female acne rose sharply from 2017 to 2020 in claims analyses. Tamoxifen and aromatase inhibitors are multi-year estrogen-receptor modulation after hormone-receptor-positive breast cancer. Male breast cancer exists and does not move a population rate. Elagolix and relugolix combinations used for endometriosis are pharmacologic menopause. User counts are smaller than the pill. Occupancy is not small.

No later section is allowed to average Tier 1 into “women take more medicines.” Men take medicines. Men do not take these medicines. A fair sex comparison of standing load begins by writing a column of zeros on the male side and then asking what else each sex takes. Exclusive occupancy also recurs in dienogest, gynecologic GnRH agents, fertility gonadotropins, and SERMs. Those classes confirm that the zero column is not an artifact of counting only the pill. They are too small to move the envelope and stay out of it. Spironolactone at acne and hirsutism doses is the same logic. In a MarketScan cohort of 38 million girls and women aged 12–40, 1.2 percent initiated the drug; 55.5 percent of initiators had acne. Initiation is not a current-stock. It stays out of the envelope. Leaving it out favors men.

4. Tier 2. Shared classes with a stable female excess

Tier 2 is the set of standing agents both sexes can receive and that women receive at higher rates. A critic who grants Tier 1 and still wants female sobriety must win here by a margin large enough to offset a column of male zeros. The published margins run the other way.

4.1 Mental health medication

The 2024 National Health Interview Survey is the lead stock. In the prior twelve months, 19.3 percent of adults took prescription medication for depression, anxiety, or other mental health reasons: 24.9 percent of women and 13.4 percent of men. The ratio is 1.86. Counseling or therapy in the same year was 16.9 percent of women and 10.9 percent of men. The narrower 2023 item, prescription medication taken specifically for depression, was 15.3 percent of women and 7.4 percent of men. White non-Hispanic adults sat at 14.3 percent on that item. The sex-by-race cell is not published in the brief. Both published margins sit above the all-adult mean of 11.4 percent. NSFG already puts White non-Hispanic women at 14.1 percent current pill use. The intersection is unpublished. The two margins are not.

IQVIA fills add the slope. From January 2016 through December 2022 the monthly antidepressant dispensing rate rose 66.3 percent. After March 2020 the slope steepened and the steepening was female. Among female adolescents the post-outbreak slope was 129.6 percent faster than the pre-outbreak slope. Male adolescent dispensing fell relative to its counterfactual. Two readings of the gap are admitted. Women report higher internalizing symptom rates, so part of the fill gap is treated morbidity. Men with comparable distress use services less. Neither reading deletes 24.9 percent. A population can need more treatment and still be more mediated. The ratio is not an American survey artifact. In England in 2023/24 the NHS Business Services Authority counted 5.71 million identified female antidepressant patients against 3.03 million male patients, a ratio of 1.88 against 1.86 in NHIS 2024.

4.2 Benzodiazepines, hypnotics, thyroid, and GLP-1

Misuse tables will not carry the anxiolytic clause. Any-use is the standing stream. NSDUH 2021 detailed tables put past-year any use of prescription benzodiazepines among adults at 11.8 percent of women and 7.9 percent of men. The 2024 NHIS sleep-aid brief is the cleanest duty-cycle document in the federal set. Any sleep aid most days: 14.8 percent of women, 10.8 percent of men. Prescription medication: 6.4 percent of women, 3.9 percent of men. Over-the-counter aids: 7.0 and 4.3 percent. Cannabis or cannabidiol as a sleep aid did not differ by sex.

Hypothyroidism treated with levothyroxine is replacement of a missing hormone, not suppression of a native cycle. It still occupies the thyroid axis with an exogenous ligand. The envelope uses the 7.5 percent seven-day female stock. A compiled census puts the female-to-male prescription ratio between 4:1 and 8:1 and a female prevalence near 11.8 percent. Optum claims placed hypothyroidism prevalence among insured women near 17.0 percent in 2019 against 5.9 percent among insured men. Autoimmune thyroiditis may explain the sex ratio. It does not convert exogenous T4 into an endogenous set point. Dropping the class is a sensitivity row. The sign survives it.

GLP-1 agonists must be split by indication. Among adults with diagnosed diabetes, NHIS 2024 found no significant sex difference in injectable GLP-1 use (27.2 percent of women, 25.9 percent of men). Among adults taking the class for weight, the sex difference is large. Gallup in 2025 put weight-loss GLP-1 use at 15.2 percent of women and 9.7 percent of men. RAND’s 2025 American Life Panel put ever-use, not current use, at 15.1 percent of women aged 30–49 against 7.2 percent of men in that band. MarketScan after the June 2021 weight-management approval of semaglutide found a female-to-male rate ratio near 2.7 overall and near 4.0 for Wegovy. Weekly administration is standing occupancy. The ligand is used to overwrite an appetite baseline. Gallup’s wording in 2025 is “taking,” not ever taking, and that current-use series is the envelope input. A 2026 Gallup follow-up put current weight-loss GLP-1 use at 11 percent of all adults, so the 2025 sex-split is not a one-survey spike.

4.3 Stimulants are not a male rescue

Adult stimulant medication was the obvious candidate for a male standing class on the CNS side. It is not. Medical Expenditure Panel Survey estimates put medical stimulant use at 2.2 percent of US adults in 2023, up from 1.8 percent in 2018. IQVIA dispensing counts show female stimulant prescriptions rose 92 percent from 2012 to 2022 against 36 percent for men. By 2022 and 2023 more stimulant prescriptions were written for female patients than for male patients. The largest increase was among women aged 35–64. The class belongs in Tier 2. It is omitted from the base envelope. Adding it as a shared, female-led fill stream would raise women at least as much as men.

5. Tier 3, the recreational ledger, male standing classes, and the envelope

5.1 Tier 3

Tier 3 exists so that disease base rates are not laundered into a prescribing morality play. Migraine is several times more common in women. Triptans follow at about 3:1. CGRP monoclonal antibodies and gepants follow harder: commercial new-user cohorts for rimegepant have been about 88 percent female. The correct entry is a high female share inside a modest population stock. Antiresorptive drugs for osteoporosis follow postmenopausal bone loss at about 4:1. None of these classes is added to the base envelope. Adding them would raise female coverage. They stay out.

5.2 Recreational events and male standing classes

Adult men still drink more heavily. Pooled NSDUH analysis for 2022–2024 placed past-month binge use at 26.0 percent of adult men and 20.0 percent of adult women. Converted to event-days the surplus is small: about sixteen ethanol event-days per adult man per year against about ten for women. The surplus changes sign before age 26. Female binge rates among adults 18–25 sat at 31.6 per 100 and male rates at 29.9 per 100 in a 2021–2023 window. Cannabis remains male among adults (25.8 versus 20.6 percent past year) and is no longer male among adolescents (12.8 versus 9.9 percent past year at ages 12–17).

Daily cannabis is standing occupancy. In 2024, 7.5 percent of people aged 12 or older used marijuana daily or almost daily in the past month. Among past-year users the daily share was 26.4 percent. Applied to the adult sex split on past-year use, that yields a working daily stock near 6.8 percent of men and 5.4 percent of women. Daily or almost daily alcohol in the same year was 3.9 percent of persons 12 or older, about 11.2 million people. That is standing ethanol, not a weekend. NSDUH did not publish the sex cell in the tables used here. The class is male-tilted in every adjacent ethanol series. It is omitted from the base envelope. It must not be added on top of the binge event-day ledger: the same daily drinkers are already inside heavy use. Substituting daily alcohol for binge event-days, at a working 5 percent of men and 3 percent of women, raises matched male coverage by a few points and does not reverse it. Table 4 prints that row.

Testosterone replacement is a male analogue in kind. Epic Research places it at 0.93 percent of male patients in 2025 and 0.95 percent in the first half of 2026. Finasteride reached about 2.5 million patients in the 2024 MEPS-derived ClinCalc series, mixed benign prostatic hyperplasia and hair, or roughly 2 percent of adult men if the denominator is forced. As-needed PDE5 inhibitors stay out as events. Daily tadalafil is the standing slice and is treated in Section 7.5. Daily cigarette use in 2024 was 7.7 percent of persons aged 12 or older and remains male-tilted. It is omitted. Adding it would raise men more than women and would still leave Table 4’s surplus intact. The male standing stack that does enter Table 4 is daily cannabis, testosterone, and finasteride, with daily alcohol as an added row so it cannot be said that ethanol was scored only as a weekend.

5.3 A first independence envelope

Working female daily-ish margins: mental health medication 0.249, hormonal contraception all-adult working share 0.09, prescription hypnotic most days 0.064, levothyroxine 0.075, weight-indication GLP-1 0.152. The independence union is approximately 0.50. Working male margins: 0.134, zero hormones, 0.039, 0.025, 0.097. The male union is approximately 0.27. Occupancy-day translations are about 183 female days and 99 male days. Benzodiazepine any-use, over-the-counter nightly aids, spironolactone, menopausal hormone therapy, tamoxifen, and daily cannabis are omitted. Adding them would raise both sides, women more than men, except for daily cannabis. The envelope is therefore a lower sketch of the female surplus, not a ceiling.

Two warnings sit on these numbers. Independence inflates unions when classes cluster in the same women. Twelve-month mental health items inflate daily stocks if short courses are common. The first warning lowers female coverage toward the largest single class, 0.249. The second warning lowers both sexes. Matched floors are 0.249 against 0.134. Matched independence envelopes are 0.50 against 0.27. Both keep the female surplus. A mixed comparison that sets the female floor against the male envelope, 0.249 against 0.27, is the one arithmetic that favors men. It is not a matched estimand. Duty-cycle-correcting the psychotropic floor before the mixed comparison is a further favor to men and is treated in Section 7.4. In ages 18–44 that mixed comparison is a tie once the floor is the hormonal term at 0.18. Matched envelopes in the same band remain 0.46 against 0.18. Population intensity in that band remains 0.58 against 0.20.

5.4 Sensitivity

The attacks on the envelope are computed in Tables 4 and 5 and Figure 9. Halving twelve-month mental health medication to proxy duty cycle leaves women at 0.42 and men at 0.21. Dropping GLP-1 leaves 0.41 and 0.19. Dropping levothyroxine, the replacement class, leaves 0.46 and 0.25. Restricting hormones to the pill, converted to an all-adult share of 0.057, leaves 0.48 and 0.27. Dropping prescription hypnotics, the class most likely nested inside the mental-health item, leaves 0.46 and 0.24. Adding daily cannabis at the working sex split, testosterone at 0.9 percent of men, and finasteride at about 2 percent of men raises both sides, women to 0.53 and men to 0.34. Substituting daily alcohol at a working 5/3 sex split on top of that stack leaves 0.54 and 0.37. Among matched estimands no row reverses the sex order. The surplus never falls below 0.17.

Mental health medication, benzodiazepines, and prescription hypnotics are not three independent classes. A woman on sertraline plus zolpidem may be one NHIS mental-health yes and a sleep-aid yes. Independence then double-counts CNS coverage. The honest floor is the largest single class: 0.249 for women, 0.134 for men. Those floors are the matched lower bounds. They are not to be compared with an independence envelope on the other sex. Trilliant’s 18–44 polypharmacy gap is the published joint the paper actually has. It runs in the same direction as the matched envelopes.

6. The reproductive-age restriction

All-adult coverage mixes two different chemical lives. After fifty, Tier 1 contraception thins and hypnotics and thyroid thicken. Before forty-five, the pill, the implant, the hormonal intrauterine system, acne-dose spironolactone, and the post-2020 psychotropic slope inhabit the same bodies. If the claim is that women are running a chemically administered youth, the test band is 18–44. Diluting that band with seventy-year-olds is how a critic makes the surplus look polite.

NSFG already lives inside 15–49. A working hormonal-methods share inside that age band, after setting aside copper devices, condoms, sterilization, and withdrawal, sits near one fifth to one quarter of the age group. NHIS 2024 puts mental health medication at 20.4 percent of adults 30–44, above ages 65–74. The 18–44 psychotropic floor is not an old-woman leftover. The sex-by-age cell is unpublished, so the restricted envelope still uses 0.249 as a female floor and 0.12 as a working male value. Trilliant’s 2024 extract found women 18–44 eight points above men 18–44 on five or more concurrent prescriptions, the widest sex gap of any age group. Men and women 65 and older converge. RAND already splits GLP-1: women 30–49 at 15.1 percent ever-use against 7.2 percent of men. Sleep-aid most-days use rises with age, so the nightly term is weaker in the test band than in the all-adult envelope.

A restricted working envelope raises hormones to 0.18, keeps female mental health medication at 0.249 as a floor, drops female prescription hypnotics to 0.03, lowers levothyroxine to 0.04, and uses a conservative GLP-1 of 0.08. Female coverage stays near 0.46. Male coverage falls to about 0.18. The ratio widens. Intensity moves more than coverage. Adding 0.18 hormonal occupancy to a psychotropic floor of 0.249 puts a large fraction of women 18–44 in a two-class world even before GLP-1 and spironolactone. Men in the same band have no such second class.

The same band is where recreational series stop rescuing men. Binge drinking among 18–25 is tied or female-tilted. Adolescent cannabis is female-tilted on several 2022–2023 measures. The post-2020 antidepressant dispensing explosion is female. White women lead the published antidepressant race margin and the published pill margin. Their intersection is unpublished. Both margins are high.

7. Objections

7.1 Help-seeking

Women use mental health services more than men at a given level of reported distress. In 2021–2023, 43 percent of females with depression and 33 percent of males with depression had seen a mental health professional in the prior year. Those facts are not denied. They do not operate as a deletion key. Composite Exogenous Load is an exposure measure. A fill is a fill. The help-seeking story also stops at the clinic door of Tier 1. No one seeks levonorgestrel because a PHQ-9 told her to. No one seeks weekly semaglutide for weight because men underuse endocrinology. An objection that explains part of one class and none of the others cannot cancel a stack.

7.2 Base rates, liberty, and the envelope

Internalizing disorders, autoimmune thyroid disease, migraine, and endometriosis are more often recorded in women. Nature can raise a base rate. It cannot rewrite the definition of unmediated baseline. An ovary producing its own estradiol is not the same object as an ovary held down by etonogestrel. A serotonergic system at its untreated set point is not the same object as a system under chronic reuptake blockade, even when the blockade is justified. If the critic’s position is that any treatment of a female-typical condition is thereby natural, the word natural has been emptied. It now means common.

Hormonal contraception is chosen. Choice is not an off-switch for occupancy. A chosen ligand still binds. One can defend access to the pill and still write that eleven percent of women 15–49 take it on a given cycle. Those two sentences have never been in conflict except among people who need them to be.

The independence envelope is not a joint. Correct. Positive correlation among classes reduces the union and concentrates intensity on the remaining heads. Population intensity is the sum of the prevalences. Linearity of expectation does not require independence. If the same women take the pill and the SSRI and the hypnotic, coverage falls from the independence sketch toward the largest margin and those women carry more classes each. That is a worse picture for unmediated baseline, not a better one. Twelve-month items are not daily items. Also correct. If every twelve-month psychotropic item were halved, female coverage would still clear male coverage on matched envelopes because Tier 1 does not halve.

7.3 Male cannabis, GLP-1 as health, and the young drinker series

Daily inhaled cannabis and daily nicotine are standing occupancies. Adult men lead both in most recent national series. Daily or almost daily alcohol, 3.9 percent of persons 12 or older in 2024, is standing ethanol. Table 4 already adds daily cannabis with testosterone and finasteride (male coverage 0.34) and then substitutes daily alcohol at a working sex split (0.37). The gap remains 0.17. Neither manufacture a male Tier 1. Weight-indication GLP-1 lowers weight in trials. That is a health argument. It is not an unmediated-baseline argument. Success on the scale is evidence of occupancy. Women 18–25 now binge at male rates. One can call that parity. Parity in event-days, laid on top of a Tier 1 and Tier 2 standing stack that men in the same band do not carry, is not a sobriety result.

Medicine is not drunkenness. An SSRI is not beer. The comparison that survives is duty cycle. Beer occupies for hours. The intrauterine system occupies for years. If the critic’s only point is lexical, the lexicon has already been replaced in Section 1.

7.4 The unmatched-bounds steelman

The strongest remaining arithmetic against the thesis is this. Put every female class inside the mental-health item, so female coverage collapses to 0.249. Leave every male class independent, so male coverage stays at 0.27. Men then lead by two points. That is the unmatched-bounds comparison: a female lower bound set against a male upper envelope. It is the comparison a critic should want. It is also a comparison of two different objects. The matched floors are 0.249 against 0.134. The matched envelopes are 0.50 against 0.27. Perfect nesting of hormones, thyroid, hypnotics, and GLP-1 inside 24.9 percent mental-health medication is not a clinical claim. It is a limit. Weight-indication GLP-1 at 15.2 percent cannot sit entirely inside a 24.9 percent psychotropic stock unless nearly every incretin user is already on a mental-health drug. Trilliant’s 18–44 polypharmacy gap already says the opposite of perfect nesting: more concurrent classes in women, not one class wearing five names.

Even if the limit is granted, the next tightening duty-cycle-corrects the psychotropic floor. Twelve-month mental health medication halved is 0.125 for women. Hormonal current use does not halve. It is already duty cycle 1. All-adult unmatched then becomes 0.125 against 0.27, a male lead of fifteen points. That row stacks three favors: collapse on women, independence on men, and a duty-cycle cut applied only to the twelve-month item. In ages 18–44 the female floor after that cut is the hormonal term, 0.18, against a male envelope of about 0.18. That is a tie on unmatched bounds. It is not a reversal of matched envelopes in the same band, which remain 0.46 against 0.18. A tie on the largest class is not a tie on the stack. Population intensity in that band is 0.58 expected classes against 0.20. Nesting does not subtract those classes. It loads them onto fewer women. Table 5 prints both unmatched rows so it cannot be said that they were hidden.

7.5 Widening the construct

A different steelman changes the mechanism test. If occupancy is any daily prescription, lisinopril, atorvastatin, and amlodipine become male-heavy standing classes in later life. The paper refused that construct in Section 2.2: a statin does not occupy monoaminergic, HPG, thyroid, incretin, or sleep circuitry as indexed here. Even under the wider construct the published joint still fails to rescue young men. Trilliant’s 18–44 polypharmacy gap is female. The 65-and-older gap closes. Widening the net moves the surplus into cardiometabolic old age and leaves the reproductive-age steroid layer untouched.

Daily tadalafil is the one PDE5 slice that belongs in a male standing stack. ClinCalc 2024 counts about 1.45 million tadalafil patients. Five milligrams, the daily BPH and daily ED dose, is half of dispensed product. On the adult-male denominator that is about 1 percent of men: smaller than finasteride, smaller than daily cannabis, and not a contraceptive layer. As-needed sildenafil remains an event class. Adding daily tadalafil to Table 4’s male standing stack moves male coverage by a point or less. It does not reverse the gap.

8. Limits of measurement and the extract that would close them

The principal hole is concurrent class membership in a thirty-day window. NHIS will say how many women took mental health medication last year. NSFG will say how many women aged 15–49 are current pill users. Neither will say how many of the second group are inside the first. Clinical eligibility criteria place no restriction on hormonal contraception for women with depressive disorders on modern antidepressants. Ordinary is not a count. MEPS prescribed-medicine files can build the cell: antidepressants, anxiolytics and hypnotics with days supplied of at least fifteen, levothyroxine, GLP-1 agonists with a weight indication, hormonal contraceptives including levonorgestrel intrauterine systems, spironolactone in women under 50 without a cardiac code, tamoxifen and aromatase inhibitors, menopausal estrogen. Outputs: coverage and intensity by sex and by ages 18–44; the four-cell hormone-by-psychotropic table; race-by-sex cells for White non-Hispanic adults.

The prediction is that positive dependence is the rule in ages 18–44. Coverage will sit closer to the psychotropic or hormonal margin than to 0.50. Intensity will sit higher. The sex order will not flip. NSFG “IUD” mixes copper and levonorgestrel. The working hormonal share of 0.18 in ages 15–49 is a judgment. If copper is a larger fraction, contraceptive occupancy falls by a few points. It does not fall to zero. Oral pills alone remain 11.4 percent of the age group. Spironolactone sits in the logic of Tier 1 and sits out of the numeric envelope until a fill rate exists. Leaving it out favors men. No table here is a plasma level or a working-memory score. The leap from “takes the agent” to “baseline is displaced” is the leap the construct requires.

9. What the sign is allowed to mean

On a random day in the mid-2020s United States, a larger share of adult women than of adult men are under standing pharmaceutical command of at least one qualifying endocrine or CNS class. The largest shared class is mental health medication, at roughly two women for every man. The largest unshared class is hormonal contraception, at several million current users against a male pharmaceutical cell of zero. Those sentences are compatible with women having higher treated internalizing prevalence and a monopoly on gestation. They are compatible with men binge drinking more after 25. Compatibility is not cancellation.

The sign does not say that every woman is mediated. A coverage near one half is also a statement that the other half is not inside the union. The sign does not say that women are drunk. The operational replacement for the slogan “socially drunk 24/7” is narrower: high duty-cycle occupancy sufficient to displace native HPG signaling and, in a large minority, monoaminergic tone and sleep initiation, on most days of the year. The sign does not say that treatment is a mistake. Better off is a clinical sentence. Unmediated is an accounting sentence. The sign does not say that men are a natural baseline. Men take nicotine, cannabis, ethanol, sleep aids, and antidepressants at half the female rate. The comparison is relative. Relative is enough to reject the claim that women are the more sober sex under this construct.

Peak intensity favors the binge. Duration favors the tablet. A year contains 8,760 hours. Sixteen binge days, even if each day is scored as fully impaired, are 384 hours. A year of an SSRI is the year. A year of a hormonal intrauterine system is the year. Once duration is admitted, the recreational surplus cannot carry the construct. Ages 18–44 are where the move has consequences beyond bookkeeping. This is the band told that it is the healthiest and most autonomous female cohort yet measured. It is also the band with a female-only steroid layer, a doubled psychotropic layer relative to same-age men, a closed or reversed binge gap, and a rising incretin rewrite. Autonomy in the political sense and mediation in the pharmacological sense are being run as if they were the same variable. They are not.

Three empirical results would force a rewrite. A claims thirty-day window in which female coverage is less than or equal to male coverage after Tier 1 is coded. A days-supplied correction that collapses twelve-month female psychotropic use so far that, even with hormones, matched female coverage falls to matched male coverage. A demonstration that most “current” hormonal users are not in a steroid state. Clinical practice makes the first conjunction unlikely. The second is the matched rows of Table 4. The third would be a survey scandal, not a reinterpretation. A tie on unmatched bounds after stacking three favors to men is not a rewrite.

10. Conclusion

Folk sobriety is a weekend. Unmediated baseline is a year. Once the second construct is the construct, the published American record of the mid-2020s does not award standing occupancy to women as the more sober sex.

Tier 1 is one-sided. The male pharmaceutical analogue of hormonal contraception is zero. Tier 2 is shared and still female, including adult stimulant fills, which by 2022–23 were written more often for women than for men. Testosterone at about 1 percent of men and finasteride at a few million patients are real male standing classes. Daily cannabis is the largest male-tilted standing recreational term. Added together they do not reverse the envelope. Sensitivity rows that halve psychotropic duty cycle, drop GLP-1, drop thyroid replacement, and restrict hormones to the pill do not reverse it either.

The licensed claim is small and hard. When sobriety is the preservation of an unmediated neurochemical and metabolic baseline, adult women carry the higher per capita Composite Exogenous Load on matched estimands. The surplus is pharmaceutical. It is thickest at ages 18–44, where even the unmatched-bounds steelman, once duty-cycle-corrected, is a tie on floors against an envelope and a female lead on matched envelopes. Occupancy is a population fact. Benefit is a clinical fact. They are not one word. The word this paper kept is baseline.
